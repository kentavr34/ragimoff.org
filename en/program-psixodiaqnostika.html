ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

What is Psychodiagnostics?

A theory course based on Freud's legacy and clinical supervision. A theory course based on Sigmund Freud's legacy and six months of clinical supervision.

The Psychodiagnostics programme has three parts: 3 months of theory (twice a week, 2 hours per class), 6 months of weekly supervision and work with Freud's texts. The tutor is Kenan Ragimov.

The theoretical part covers the conceptual apparatus of psychoanalysis: the unconscious, resistance, dream interpretation, slips of the tongue, defence mechanisms, infantile sexuality, transference and countertransference. In the supervision block participants work with their own clinical material.

Programme bonus: all 24 books by Sigmund Freud, translated from the original German into Azerbaijani for the first time in Azerbaijan, will be available to the students of this programme.
