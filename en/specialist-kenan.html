Kenan Ragimov

Professional journey

  1. 2026

    Clinical Psychiatry — Reference Handbook

    Author of the Clinical Psychiatry handbook — a comprehensive reference on diagnosis and treatment of psychological disorders. DSM-5, ICD-11, updated protocols 2026.

    Read the handbook →
  2. 2026

    Sigmund Freud — in Azerbaijani

    A first for Azerbaijan — the translation of all of Sigmund Freud’s books into Azerbaijani.

    See the books →
  3. 2025

    Schizophrenia: the pandemic of madness

    A book on the clinical and psychological picture of psychosis.

    See the book →
  4. 2024

    The guilt virus

    A book about freeing yourself from the guilt that ruins a life.

    See the book →
  5. 2022 — present

    IPAS Ambassador

    Certified representative of the International Psychotherapy Association. Graduates receive an IPAS certificate with a QR verification code.

  6. 2021 — present

    Karvasarsky Institute — Representative

    Continuing professional development programme for clinical psychologists. No nostrification required.

  7. 2020 — present

    RAGIMOFF Psychology School

    Translation of ICD-11 into Azerbaijani. Development of the General Psychotherapy textbook. Training of professional specialists.

  8. 2017 — present

    Ragimoff LP — Scotland

    Garnet development programme. International HR projects.

  9. 2012 — present

    Ragimoff HK Ltd. — Hong Kong

    Film producer, training of HR and marketing specialists, business development consulting.

  10. 2008 — present

    Global Training and Consulting (GTC) — Baku

    Founder. HR School (2000+ graduates), Sales School. Over 3,000 participants. From 2019 — state certificate IKTLAB.

  11. 2006

    Bank of Baku — Assessment of 232 Employees

    Full assessment completed in 1 day. An internal assessment programme was created based on the results. An updated version was produced in 2011.

  12. 2004 — 2005

    Military Unit, Ganja — Head of Medical Post

    Awarded a certificate of honour. Military rank — senior lieutenant.

  13. 2003 — 2008

    Regional Psychology Centre — Director

    Regular expert appearances on psychology topics on TV and radio. Clinical work in collaboration with the Vital clinic.

  14. 2001 — 2003

    Bekhterev Institute, St. Petersburg

    Clinical residency under Prof. B. D. Karvasarsky. Diploma with distinction. Additional training at IMATON, Baskova and FINEC centres.

  15. 1994 — 2001

    Azerbaijan State Medical University

    Faculty of General Medicine. Speciality: psychiatrist. Internship at Clinical Psychiatric Hospital No. 2.
