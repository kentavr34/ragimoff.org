TRAINING PROGRAMME

Family Therapy

Two modules, three months each: study, practice, supervision. Two modules. Each lasts 3 months: 2 months of study and practice, the 3rd month is supervision. The price is the same in person and online.

Classes run twice a week, two hours each.

Couple Therapy

Man-woman relationships

Work with the couple system: from assessment to the crisis stage. Intake, genogram, roles and boundaries, patterns, the approach to infidelity and impending divorce.

3 months 16 lessons + supervision twice a week — Tuesday and Thursday, 14:00–16:00; the training starts on 1 September

Total 600 AZN — 200 AZN a month × 3 months, or 500 AZN paid in advance. The price is the same in person and online.

CONSULTATION

Couple Therapy

A joint intake, then an individual stage, then together again. The work starts with a joint intake, continues individually and joins again.

Therapy is led by two specialists: the man’s side by psychiatrist Kenan Rahimov, the woman’s side by clinical psychologist Samira Rahimova. The work begins with a joint intake: both sides are heard in the same room and the situation is assessed together. Then comes the individual stage — each person explores their own part in a safe setting rather than in front of the partner. In the closing joint session the results are brought together and agreements are made. A session lasts one hour, in person or online. The full description of the process and the prices are on a separate page.
