Short programme

Psychoanalysis

A theory course based on Sigmund Freud’s legacy and six months of clinical supervision. The theoretical part covers the conceptual apparatus of psychoanalysis: the unconscious, resistance, dream interpretation, slips of the tongue, defence mechanisms, infantile sexuality, transference and countertransference.

Duration
3 months of theory + 6 months of group supervision
Volume
48 hours of theory + 24 supervision sessions
Format
In person or online; class recordings available without limit
Start
Enrolment is open
Document
International IPAS certificate

Register

Who it is for

  • Psychologists, psychotherapists, doctors and educators
  • Psychology students and early-career specialists
  • Those ready to work with Freud’s texts
  • Higher education is not required; those with a background in psychology have wider opportunities

Who teaches

Kenan Ragimovtutor and head of the programme; author of the project translating Freud’s legacy into Azerbaijani

What is included

  • Bonus: all 25 books by Sigmund Freud, translated from the original German into Azerbaijani for the first time in Azerbaijan, will be available to the students of this programme
  • Unlimited viewing of class video recordings
  • Small groups — individual attention for every participant
  • Attestation at the end of the programme

Curriculum

Module 1 — Theory · 3 months (2×2 hours weekly)
  • The unconscious, the topographical model of the mind, resistance · 16 hrs
  • Dream interpretation, slips, symbol and language · 16 hrs
  • Defence mechanisms, infantile sexuality, transference and countertransference · 16 hrs
Module 2 — Supervision · 6 months (once a week)
  • Weekly supervision sessions based on the participant’s own clinical material (material is anonymised and discussed in the group) · 24 sessions

Document

Certificate of the International Psychotherapy Association (IPAS)

The programme is run by RAGIMOFF; graduates receive a certificate of the International Psychotherapy Association. Its authenticity can be checked with the QR code on it or on the association’s website (intpas.com).
