Literature and Sources

This list presents the key sources referenced in the book, as well as literature used by the author in preparing the text. The bibliography is organized thematically, with subsections arranged, as a rule, chronologically (for historical works) or alphabetically (for contemporary sources). For publicly available documents, direct links are provided as they appeared during manuscript preparation.

The majority of cited works come from internationally peer-reviewed scientific literature, documents from professional associations (WPA, APA, NICE, WHO), judicial rulings, and open archival materials. For certain historical subjects, original primary publications in German, French, and English were used in their unedited form.
