Short programme

Family therapy

Couple relationships, the parent-child bond and work with difficult adolescents — therapy and training. Two modules: “Couple Therapy” and “Parent-Child Relationships”.

Duration
3 months of training + 6 months of group supervision
Volume
Each module: 48 hours of classes (twice a week, 2 hours each) and 48 hours of supervision (once a week); 16 lessons per module
Format
In person or online (same price); Tuesday and Thursday, 14:00–16:00
Start
Enrolment is open
Document
IPAS certificate (the course ends with an exam)

Register

Who it is for

  • Psychologists and school psychologists
  • Teachers and coaches
  • Students
  • Parents
  • A degree is not required

Who teaches

Samira Ragimovaleads the training: couple relationships, the parent-child bond, difficult adolescents

What is included

  • Video recordings of the training — you can watch a class by each trainer in advance
  • 6 months of supervision, once a week
  • An exam and a certificate from the International Psychotherapy Association at the end of the course

Curriculum

Module 1 — Couple Therapy (man-woman relationships)
  • Identifying disharmony in the family system — assessment
  • The intake session in couple therapy
  • Analysis of the conflict situation
  • The genogram — the family life cycle
  • Defining roles in the family system and strained boundaries
  • Psychodrama — psychotherapeutic techniques in the family model
  • Unwritten rules — sharing obligations and the cost of breaking them
  • Identifying patterns in the relationship
  • Applying methods from classical and contemporary schools of family therapy
  • The range of problems systemic family therapy addresses
  • Meeting personal needs and freedom of choice
  • Methods of communication (G. Bateson’s theory)
  • Psychotherapeutic techniques in family relationships and worked cases
  • Work in a critical period (infidelity, abuse, the approach on the verge of divorce)
  • Analysing the relationship with a person suffering from addiction (alcohol, drugs, gambling and so on)
  • The supportive stage in family therapy
Module 2 — Parent-Child Relationships (family therapy for parents)
  • Stages of a child’s psychophysiological development
  • Critical points in the stages of development
  • Symptom-based assessment in family therapy
  • Functional disorders within the family
  • Systemic analysis of family stories
  • Building a model of communication with the child
  • Ways out of strained relationships, correcting parental methods
  • Children’s behaviour patterns in troubled families
  • Neurotic and behavioural disorders in children
  • Play therapy — correcting the behaviour model
  • Aggressive behaviour in children — the punishment model
  • Children’s fears and practical ways out
  • Problems of falling behind in schooling
  • Regulating a child’s psyche in a single-parent family
  • Methods of sex education in the family
  • Managing family relationships with a person who has a specific illness or behavioural limitation

Document

Certificate of the International Psychotherapy Association (IPAS)

The programme is run by RAGIMOFF; graduates receive a certificate of the International Psychotherapy Association. Its authenticity can be checked with the QR code on it or on the association’s website (intpas.com).
