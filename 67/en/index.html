Myths and facts

What they say — and what is true

The source is given. You can verify it.

“Melipramine builds an awakening reflex”

Fact: In fact: it does not build a reflex — it delays urination as a side effect. Cardiotoxic; deaths are known. Not recommended for childhood enuresis.

Source: “Clinical Psychiatry”, enuresis section

“Enurexin can be used for enuresis”

Fact: In fact: documents of the Ministry of Health of Azerbaijan do not recommend it in paediatrics for nocturnal enuresis.

Source: Ministry of Health PDF

“Acupuncture cures enuresis”

Fact: In fact: there is no proven effect on cognition or behaviour. Efficacy in a psychological disorder is not confirmed.

Source: international protocols and consensus
