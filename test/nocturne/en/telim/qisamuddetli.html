Psychodiagnostics

Programme: three months

  1. Month 1

    General psychology

    The concept of mental functions; the different mental functions and how they are measured.

  2. Month 2

    Psychopathology

    Psychopathological symptoms — disorders of attention, memory and other functions; clinical syndromes.

  3. Month 3

    Diagnosis by ICD

    Based on the textbook “Clinical Psychiatry”: chapters, symptoms, justifying the diagnosis, methods of diagnosis and confirmation, recommended therapy.

3 months of training + 6 months of group supervision.
