Official Documents

CPD Diploma & International Certificate

An official diploma and certificates from the St. Petersburg Institute of Additional Professional Education for Psychologists and Psychotherapists.

Legal Status of the Diploma

Under the Agreement between the Russian Federation and the Republic of Azerbaijan in the field of education (AR Law No. 455-IIQ of 13 May 2003), the diploma of the St. Petersburg Institute of Additional Professional Education for Psychologists and Psychotherapists is an officially recognised document in Azerbaijan. Graduates — subject to employer requirements — may work in both state and private institutions.
