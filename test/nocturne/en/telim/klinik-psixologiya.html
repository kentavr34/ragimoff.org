ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

What is Clinical Psychology CPD?

23 disciplines, 5 blocks — an official CPD diploma in the FRDO registry and the right to independent clinical practice.

The Clinical Psychology CPD programme covers the diagnosis of mental disorders using DSM-5 and ICD-11, core psychotherapeutic approaches (CBT, elements of EMDR), and real clinical practice. The programme is designed for professionals already working in their field or planning to start.

Upon completing the one-year programme, graduates receive a CPD diploma entered into the FRDO state registry. The diploma is officially recognised and grants the right to independent practice in complex clinical cases.

In addition, graduates receive an international IPAS (International Psychotherapy Accreditation Society) certificate — a QR-verified document valid outside Azerbaijan.

  • A higher education degree is required (any field)
  • Full coverage of DSM-5 / ICD-11 diagnostic systems
  • Practical application of CBT protocols
  • PTSD, trauma, and child psychology modules
  • Real clinical case analysis under supervision
  • CPD diploma + international IPAS certificate — two credentials at once

PROGRAMME HIGHLIGHTS

What to Expect

01 — Theory

Video Lessons + Literature

Video recordings of Dr. Ragimov's classes are available for every discipline. You can watch them anytime, from anywhere — through your personal student account. A recommended professional reading list is also provided.

02 — Practicum

Live Practice with Dr. Ragimov

Live demonstration sessions are held every week: real clinical material, supervision, and case analysis. Knowing the theory is not enough — developing clinical thinking requires working alongside an experienced professional. This programme takes you from "I know" to "I can do."

03 — Official Status

CPD Diploma + IPAS Certificate

A CPD diploma is issued and entered into the FRDO state registry in accordance with the legislation of the Republic of Azerbaijan. No legalisation required. Employers may verify it through the FIS FRDO registry. In addition, graduates receive an international IPAS/BPA certificate.

Questions About the Diploma's Legal Status?

Under the Agreement between the Russian Federation and the Republic of Azerbaijan in the field of education (Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan No. 455-IIQ, 13 May 2003), the diploma of the St. Petersburg Institute of Additional Professional Education for Psychologists and Psychotherapists is officially recognised in Azerbaijan. Graduates may work in both public and private institutions.
