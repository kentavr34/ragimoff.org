ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

What is Psychoanalysis?

A theory course based on Sigmund Freud's legacy and six months of clinical supervision.

The Psychoanalysis programme has three parts: 3 months of theory (twice a week, 2 hours per class), 6 months of weekly supervision and work with Freud's texts. The tutor is Kenan Ragimov.

The theoretical part covers the conceptual apparatus of psychoanalysis: the unconscious, resistance, dream interpretation, slips of the tongue, defence mechanisms, infantile sexuality, transference and countertransference. In the supervision block participants work with their own clinical material.

Programme bonus: all 24 books by Sigmund Freud, translated from the original German into Azerbaijani for the first time in Azerbaijan, will be available to the students of this programme.

  • Tutor and supervisor — Kenan Ragimov

  • 6 months of supervision: weekly, based on the participant’s own clinical material

Tutor

Kenan Ragimov — author and supervisor

The programme is led by Kenan Ragimov: 23 years of clinical practice and author of the project translating Freud’s legacy into Azerbaijani. Theory classes and supervision are held under his personal guidance.
