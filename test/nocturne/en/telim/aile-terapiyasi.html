FAMILY THERAPY

Systemic Family Therapy

Couple relationships, the parent-child bond and work with difficult adolescents — therapy and training.

Led bySamira RahimovaCouple relationships, the parent-child bond and work with difficult adolescents — therapy and training.About the specialist →
TRAINING PROGRAMME

Family Therapy

Two modules: 3 months of classes — twice a week, two hours each — and 6 months of supervision, once a week. The price is the same in person and online.

Classes run twice a week — Tuesday and Thursday, 14:00–16:00. Registration is open.

Module 1

Couple Therapy

Man-woman relationships

Work with the couple system: from assessment to the crisis stage. Intake, genogram, roles and boundaries, patterns, the approach to infidelity and impending divorce.

3 months of classes + 6 months of supervision 16 lessons twice a week × 2 hours

Total for the programme: 900 AZN — 3 months of classes and 6 months of supervision included; payable at 300 AZN a month × 3 months, or 800 AZN with prepayment. The price is the same in person and online.

See the full programme
  1. Identifying disharmony in the family system — assessment
  2. The intake session in couple therapy
  3. Analysis of the conflict situation
  4. The genogram — the family life cycle
  5. Defining roles in the family system and strained boundaries
  6. Psychodrama — psychotherapeutic techniques in the family model
  7. Unwritten rules — sharing obligations and the cost of breaking them
  8. Identifying patterns in the relationship
  9. Applying methods from classical and contemporary schools of family therapy
  10. The range of problems systemic family therapy addresses
  11. Meeting personal needs and freedom of choice
  12. Methods of communication (G. Bateson’s theory)
  13. Psychotherapeutic techniques in family relationships and worked cases
  14. Work in a critical period (infidelity, abuse, the approach on the verge of divorce)
  15. Analysing the relationship with a person suffering from addiction (alcohol, drugs, gambling and so on)
  16. The supportive stage in family therapy

Video recordings of the training — you can watch a class by each trainer in advance:Dr. Ragimov’s class Samira Ragimova’s class

Module 2

Parent-Child Relationships

Family therapy for parents

Stages of a child’s development and its critical points, intra-family functional disorders, building a model of communication, play therapy, fears and aggression. For psychologists, teachers and parents.

3 months of classes + 6 months of supervision 16 lessons twice a week × 2 hours

Total for the programme: 900 AZN — 3 months of classes and 6 months of supervision included; payable at 300 AZN a month × 3 months, or 800 AZN with prepayment. The price is the same in person and online.

See the full programme
  1. Stages of a child’s psychophysiological development
  2. Critical points in the stages of development
  3. Symptom-based assessment in family therapy
  4. Functional disorders within the family
  5. Systemic analysis of family stories
  6. Building a model of communication with the child
  7. Ways out of strained relationships, correcting parental methods
  8. Children’s behaviour patterns in troubled families
  9. Neurotic and behavioural disorders in children
  10. Play therapy — correcting the behaviour model
  11. Aggressive behaviour in children — the punishment model
  12. Children’s fears and practical ways out
  13. Problems of falling behind in schooling
  14. Regulating a child’s psyche in a single-parent family
  15. Methods of sex education in the family
  16. Managing family relationships with a person who has a specific illness or behavioural limitation

The programme is for psychologists, school psychologists, teachers, coaches, students and parents; a degree is not required. The course ends with an exam and a certificate from the International Psychotherapy Accreditation Society.
