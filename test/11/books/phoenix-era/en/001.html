You are the Master of your own destiny.

Dear reader,

If you are holding this book in your hands, a time of searching has come into your life. Perhaps you feel that something is going wrong in your relationships with those close to you. Perhaps you look around and see many unhappy people running in circles, not knowing where the way out is. Or maybe you simply want to understand why, even with every material comfort, a person remains lonely inside.

I want to tell you one thing that can change everything: you are the author of your life. You are the Master of your own destiny.

The world of relationships is in chaos not because fate is written in the stars or in our genes. Chaos arises because people have forgotten: they are the ones steering their own lives. The Phoenix is not a mythical bird — it is a state of mind in a person who sees connections as white threads linking their beliefs, their behavior, and their attitude toward people.

When you become aware of these threads, you begin to build relationships on a healthy foundation. Priorities shift. At the start of the journey, sincerity matters. Along the way, what matters is mercy. When a person has sincerity, love, and mercy, they live as a Phoenix. They rewrite the history of their relationships, leaving grievances and neurotic scenarios in the past, and build their future with ease.

What awaits you in this book?

A fascinating journey of transformation awaits you. From the moment you turn the first page, you will begin to change. This is not just a work of non-fiction but a guide to action, filtered through the lens of real human destinies.

To make it more interesting for you — so the theory isn’t left hanging in the air — we will share with you the story of Maria. Maria is a composite figure. She is a virtual traveling companion, created from the fates of hundreds of people who have walked this path in real life. Yes, a person named Maria exists, but everything described here is a synthesis of experience, an attempt to convey the essence to you through the story of one soul. Don’t worry too much about her misfortunes — instead, try to understand why they happened. That way you will see how scientific knowledge shapes real life.

The journey begins. This is the most fascinating world there is — the world of spiritual growth. We will travel through it together, from the shadow of Lilith to the light of the Phoenix.

The information you will find here is based on facts and on the experience of hundreds of people who have walked this path. We do not offer ready-made solutions. We offer a map. And the road — you choose that yourself. We have added historical parallels, psychological grounding, and examples so that you don’t just memorize the terms but feel what they mean.

Get ready. Your transformation starts now.
