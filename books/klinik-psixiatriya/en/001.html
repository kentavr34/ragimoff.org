A word from the author

This book is intended for those studying or working in mental health, and for anyone who simply wants to understand a topic for themselves. The goal is simple: when you open any disorder's page, you should find not an opinion from social media, but a verified guide — diagnosis, treatment, and a review of the most common misconceptions — drawn from current international sources, each claim traceable to where it came from.

The online edition is free to use, with no obligation of any kind. But if the book has been useful to you and you would like to support its continued development, you can order a printed copy in your language — I will personally sign and mail it to you, wherever you are.

Dr. Kenan Ragimov
