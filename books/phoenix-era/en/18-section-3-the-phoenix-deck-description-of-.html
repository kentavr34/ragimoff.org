Section 3. The “PHOENIX” deck: description of the cards

3.1. Character cards (24 in total)

Each archetype is represented by two cards — a light side and a dark side. All 24 character cards are used in play.

STRENGTHARCHETYPELIGHT SIDEDARK SIDE
1Infanta“I can’t, you decide for me.” Total helplessness, waiting for someone to come along and fix everything.“You must take control of me, or I’ll be lost.” Manipulation through weakness, sulking, tears.
2Beggar“Help me, I have nothing.” Openly demanding help, playing the victim.“You all owe me for my suffering.” Aggressive demands, guilt-tripping.
3Puttana“Burned out, gone into the shadows.” Vanishing, walking away after a rebellion.“I’ll destroy everything you’ve built.” Aggressive rebellion, scandal, provocation.
4Cinderella“I serve so that I’ll be loved.” Self-sacrifice, working for gratitude.“I’ve done so much, and you don’t appreciate me.” Resentment, demanding recognition.
5Mistress“I love him, so I’ll forgive everything.” Patience, devotion, waiting.“You won’t leave, I couldn’t take it.” Emotional blackmail, threats, hysterics.
6Hippie“I live here and now, without commitments.” Freedom, lightness, creativity.“I don’t need anything, leave me alone.” Withdrawing into nothing, dependence, degradation.
7Courtesan“I’m free, I choose.” Independence, play, pleasure.“You’re just a trophy.” Cynicism, devaluing your partners.
8Lilith“Money solves everything.” Rationality, calculation, security.“I’ll buy you, and you’ll serve me.” Power through resources, humiliation.
9Geisha“My talent serves people.” Creativity, inspiration, beauty.“Admirers are my drug.” Pride, addiction to praise.
10Teresa“I help because I can.” Sincere service, mercy.“You all must love me for my saintliness.” Manipulation through self-sacrifice.
11Chameleon“I understand everyone, I’m flexible.” Diplomacy, empathy, adaptation.“I’m nobody, I’m just a mirror.” Losing yourself, inner emptiness.
12Phoenix“I am sincere, I love, I am free.” Wholeness, maturity, responsibility.The dark side is absent — this is the peak of development.

3.2. Situation cards (24 in total)

The situation cards set the context for play in the first round. The author reads out the situation, and the players respond according to their hidden character card. Bonuses are awarded based on the results of the round.
