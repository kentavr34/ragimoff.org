Afterword · A message to the traveler

“Knowledge is fire. You can burn yourself, you can warm yourself, you can light the way. The choice belongs to the one who holds the torch.”

Dear reader,

You’ve turned the last page. The text is over. But it’s precisely in this silence, between the final period and the closing of the cover, that the most important thing begins.

Remember the beginning of the journey? We talked about how you are the Master of your own destiny. Back then, it might have sounded like a pretty metaphor, like a promise made by a stranger on a train. But now, having passed through the labyrinth of twelve models, having seen the faces of Lilith, having heard the cry of Puttana, having felt the comfortable mask of the Chameleon, you know: this is not a metaphor. This is the anatomy of your soul.

We gave you a map. We showed you where the swamps of neuroses lie, where the mirages of safety hide, and where the territory of freedom begins. But a map is not the territory. A book is not life.

As the French writer Bernard Werber wrote: “Death is not the end, it’s simply a transition to the next level”. I will put it differently: Birth is not the beginning. The beginning is the moment when you stop being what circumstances made you.

Right now you are standing on a threshold. You hold a key in your hands. It is not the book. It is awareness. You can close it and put it on a shelf. Tomorrow morning you’ll wake up, drink your coffee, put on the usual mask, and head to work. You’ll play the role of the Chameleon so that people love you. Or the role of Cinderella so that no one leaves you. That’s the safe path. The path millions choose. The path where ashes remain ashes.

Or you can take a step into the fire.

In the Appendix, you found the rules of the game. But the most important game doesn’t happen at a table with cards. It happens here. In your chest. In the moment when you want to lie so you look better. In the moment when you want to make demands because you feel like a victim. In the moment when it’s easier to stay silent than to speak the truth.

In that moment, remember the Phoenix.

The Phoenix is not afraid to burn. It knows: ashes are not the end of the story. They are fertilizer for new life. Every time you choose sincerity over convenience, you burn off an old skin. Every time you choose love over safety, you grow a new wing.

We built the School. We wrote the method. We designed the game. But all of it exists only if you want it to. Don’t wait for permission. You are already the Master. Don’t fear mistakes. A mistake is simply experience that will become part of your feathers.

The world has too many people playing roles. The world is suffocating under masks. It doesn’t need another reflection in a crooked mirror. It needs your light. The real one. Not polished, not safe — alive.

So what comes next?

Close the book. Look in the mirror. Not at the clothes, not at the hair. Into the eyes. Ask yourself: “Who is looking back at me?”. If you see exhaustion there — give yourself rest. If you see fear there — take a step through it. If you see fire there — don’t put it out.

Go where your soul burns. Find your people. The ones who are also tired of masks. Come to the game. Become a Trainer. Become a Master. Or simply become Yourself.

P.S. Important:

This book is not an advertisement for the school and not an invitation to join a community. It was conceived as a standalone tool. If you read it, took the test, played with friends, and changed your life — that is the main result.

Everything else — the School, game practices, training — exists only for those who need it. And does not exist for those who don’t. Choose what resonates. Your path is yours alone.

The Phoenix Era does not arrive by the calendar. It arrives when at least one person decides to stop being ashes.

You are that person.

Take flight.

The End of This Book.
