SECTION 4. THE ETHICAL CODE OF A CONSULTANT OF THE “PHOENIX” SCHOOL · Core Principles

PRINCIPLECONTENT
1. SafetyThe space of the game and of therapy is protected. No violence, no judgment, no manipulation. Every participant has the right to say “no” and leave the game without giving a reason.
2. ConfidentialityWhatever is said within the circle stays within the circle. The only exception: a threat to life or a criminal offense.
3. Boundaries of competenceThe consultant (Trainer) works with relationship models, helping people analyze and change their behavior. Signs of depression, anxiety disorders, or psychosis are grounds for referring the client to a clinical psychologist.
4. Refusal of manipulationIt is forbidden to use the methodology for personal gain, to pressure clients, or to create dependencies.
5. Transparent feesThe cost of participating in a group is announced before the work begins. No hidden payments or obligations.
6. Non-discriminationAny form of discrimination based on gender, age, nationality, religion, or sexual orientation is prohibited.
7. Responsibility to the communityThe Trainer is required to attend supervision with the Master at least twice a year, so as not to burn out and not to lose objectivity.
