Section 5. The therapeutic personal development program

The Phoenix School: from the game to life

The game is the front door. Beyond it, a long corridor of transformation begins. The Phoenix School of Methodology offers a systematic personal development program for those ready to walk the path all the way to the end.

The philosophy of the School: we don’t teach people to be “proper.” We help them be real. Our goal is not to fit someone into a standard, but to reveal a person’s unique essence by freeing them from neurotic shackles.

Structure of the training

LEVELFOR WHOMPROGRAMRESULT
Level 1 Student (Awareness)For those who have played the game and want to understand themselves more deeply.• Diagnostics: in-depth testing, building a map of the personality. • Theory: studying the 12 relationship models, seminars. • Practice: attending games, keeping a self-observation journal.A clear understanding of your dominant Model and your zones of growth.
Level 2 Trainer (Transformation)For those who want to help others and deepen their own therapy.• A 6-month course on facilitating games and group dynamics. • Personal work with a Master (one-on-one and in groups). • Practice: facilitating your first games under supervision.The Trainer certificate and the right to facilitate games of the First and Second Rounds.
Level 3 Master (Service)For professional psychologists and transformation leaders.• Advanced study of therapeutic techniques. • Mentorship: supervising Trainers. • Contribution: developing scenarios, participating in the School’s Councils.The title of Master and the right to lead therapeutic groups.
Level 4 Grandmaster (Light)The highest level, conferred by the Council of the School.• Mission: safeguarding the methodology, developing the School. • Responsibility: approving new Masters, ethical oversight.The right to represent the School in the world, and access to closed materials.

How to begin the path

1. Read the book — it’s your map of the terrain.

2. Find a game — attend an open game session of The Phoenix Era in your city or online.

3. Take the diagnostic — learn your current Model (100 questions in Section 2).

4. Join a group — begin therapy with a certified Trainer or Master.

5. Become the light — complete the training and start helping others find their wings.

“The Phoenix is not afraid to burn. It knows: ashes are not the end of the story. They are the fertilizer for new life.

Welcome to The Phoenix Era.”
