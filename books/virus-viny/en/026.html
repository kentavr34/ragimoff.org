B. A LETTER TO YOURSELF (CFT TEMPLATE)

Dear [Your Name],

I know things are hard for you right now. You’re feeling [describe the emotion].

That’s okay. You have every right to feel this way.

You’re not alone. Many people go through similar experiences.

You’re not a bad person. You made a mistake, and that can be fixed.

I’m here with you. I see you. I’ve got your back.

You’re safe. You are good enough.

With love,

[Your Name]

Instructions: Write this letter to yourself once a week. Read it aloud.
