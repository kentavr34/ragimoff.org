About the book

Crisis, stress and rebirth.

Phoenix Era is a collection of essays born from the author's 23 years of clinical practice. Based on real patient stories, psychological observations and personal reflections, the book serves as a psychological map for navigating crisis, stress, and periods of change in a healthy way.

This book is for those who want to become psychologists, for those seeking psychological help, and for those on a path of self-discovery. Each essay is a lesson filtered through years of clinical practice.

The theme of the Phoenix Era is inner strength, motivation, and psychological resilience. The book tells the reader "you can rise too" — with professional clinical support, real stories, and a psychological framework.

Sample

«Under the Ashes» — first essay.

Ashes are not always warm. Sometimes they're simply the cold trace of ash wind — time, events, broken hopes. We call these traces "crisis". But crisis is not just an end. It's also a beginning.

The main question: do you look under the ashes, or do you rise above them and watch the winds? If you look — you're preparing for fire. If you rise — you're preparing to become a Phoenix.

In clinical practice I've seen that the strongest people are those who found the courage to look under the ashes — and learned to see the remaining embers. Because every ember is a Phoenix. Every ember is a sprout of a new beginning.

— Kenan Ragimov, Phoenix Era
